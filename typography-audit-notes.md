# Technical Typography Audit

All seven routes were reviewed at desktop and mobile sizes: Home, Downsizing Services, Aging in Place, Buying & Selling, Resource Center, Meet Mary, and Contact.

The screenshots did not show a clear missing-space character defect in the supplied example phrases, broken glyphs, or distorted photography. The recurring technical risk is large editorial headings wrapping too tightly at narrow widths and long CTA labels creating cramped inline rows. Any correction must remain CSS-only and limited to line-height, heading width, wrapping, and flex/grid spacing. No copy, layout structure, colors, imagery, navigation, footer, CTA strategy, icons, or intentional empty states should change.
