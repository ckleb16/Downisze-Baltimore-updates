# Downsize Baltimore — Design Direction

## Three Directions Considered

### Theme Name: Harbor House Editorial
Very Brief Intro: A quiet, premium editorial system that pairs Baltimore navy with warm paper tones and restrained gold details. It makes practical guidance feel personal, grounded, and considered.
Probability: 0.06

### Theme Name: Modern Townhouse
Very Brief Intro: A brighter, more architectural direction using crisp white space, structured framing, and a contemporary real-estate visual language. It would feel more design-forward and less intimate.
Probability: 0.03

### Theme Name: Evening Welcome
Very Brief Intro: A darker, atmospheric direction built around the warmth of a lit doorway at dusk, with deeper navy fields and copper-toned accents. It would create more drama but less openness.
Probability: 0.08

## Chosen Approach: Harbor House Editorial

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial design with the warmth of a boutique lifestyle publication and the clarity of a trusted local advisor.

### Core Principles
- Make the page feel calm before it feels clever.
- Use generous margins and asymmetric composition to create room for decisions.
- Let navy carry authority, cream carry hospitality, and gold mark moments of movement.
- Keep nautical references occasional and symbolic rather than thematic.

### Color Philosophy
The palette is built around Baltimore navy (#102A43), soft cream (#F5F1E9), warm white (#FCFBF8), and antique gold (#B48A4A). Navy creates confidence without looking corporate. Cream softens the page and makes practical information feel human. Gold is reserved for rules, small labels, and active states so it reads as a quiet signal of possibility rather than decoration.

### Layout Paradigm
Use a magazine-like vertical rhythm: a strong full-bleed image to establish place, followed by offset editorial sections where text and visual blocks are intentionally not centered in a uniform grid. Cards should feel like gathered notes or field guides, with a strong left edge and varied internal alignment.

### Signature Elements
1. Fine gold rules and micro-labels that act as quiet editorial punctuation.
2. A small compass-house mark used as a recurring navigational cue.
3. Cream paper textures and navy contour-line fields used sparingly to add tactility.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like opening a well-made folder: direct, calm, and reassuring. Buttons lift slightly on hover, card borders warm to gold, navigation highlights are underlined rather than filled, and the mobile menu opens as a full-width cream panel with clear hierarchy.

### Animation
Use short, low-drama transitions. Hero content enters with a gentle upward reveal, staggered by 60ms. Resource cards translate upward by 4px and warm their border on hover. Decorative contour lines remain static. Respect reduced-motion preferences by disabling entrance transforms and keeping only color transitions.

### Typography System
Use Cormorant Garamond for display headlines and section titles, with DM Sans for navigation, body copy, labels, and buttons. Headlines should be large, high-contrast, and sentence case; body copy should remain readable at 17–19px with generous line height. Small labels use uppercase DM Sans with increased tracking.

### Brand Essence
Downsize Baltimore is a thoughtful local guide for Baltimore-area families making housing decisions later in life, offering clarity and connections without pressure. Personality: grounded, warm, discerning.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are clear, humane, and quietly confident. CTAs sound like an invitation, not a funnel. Microcopy acknowledges uncertainty and makes the next step feel manageable.

Example lines:
- “You don’t have to know the answer yet.”
- “Let’s make a plan that fits the life you’re actually living.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a wide wordmark treatment with a compact house-and-compass symbol. The symbol should work independently in the footer and favicon, while the header uses the symbol beside a carefully spaced uppercase wordmark.

### Signature Brand Color
Baltimore Navy #102A43 — a deep, ink-like blue that feels local, capable, and quietly protective.

## Implementation Reminder
Every page and component should reinforce Harbor House Editorial through the navy/cream/gold palette, editorial type pairing, asymmetric spacing, fine rules, and restrained motion. When in doubt, ask: “Does this make the decision feel clearer and the brand feel more human?”

## Buying & Selling Page Refinements

The Buying & Selling page is an execution-led companion to the planning-oriented Downsizing Services page. It keeps the Downsize Baltimore navy, soft cream, antique gold, Cormorant Garamond, and DM Sans system while using more decisive rules, numbered transaction phases, and a split editorial feature opening.

The supplied Downsize Baltimore logo remains the established brand mark. The surrounding system treats the header as editorial wayfinding rather than a portal menu, using spacious navy navigation with fine gold underlines instead of boxed tabs. The supplied Mary portrait anchors the page’s warm, human visual story, while fine gold rules, section numerals, and restrained contour fields carry the page from hero through the final invitation. The opening composition remains intentionally asymmetric through an offset portrait panel, a vertical caption, and copy positioned slightly above the image center.

## Resource Center Refinements

The Resource Center uses spacious editorial wayfinding on navy rather than boxed portal tabs. Its six pathways read as curated field-guide notes through strong left edges, fine gold rules, section numbers, and small icon punctuation. Compass-house cues appear at key transitions and in the final invitation, remaining symbolic and restrained. The page stays question-led, educational, and low-pressure, with development labels used honestly for resources not yet published.

## Meet Mary Refinements

The Meet Mary page uses open editorial wayfinding on navy rather than boxed navigation cells. Antique gold is reserved for section labels, emotional punctuation, rules, and primary actions, while navy carries more headline authority. The biography is composed as a feature profile with offset text blocks and varied section relationships. Nautical cues remain symbolic, so the page reads first as Baltimore-local, personal, and protective rather than maritime-themed.

## Pass 2 Visual Branding Decisions

The anchor is now the primary subtle decorative motif where a compass had been used ornamentally. Functional subject icons remain functional. Antique gold remains restrained, while selected neutral sections use cleaner cream to keep the system warm without becoming muddy. Existing photography, copy, layout structure, and the human editorial tone remain unchanged.
