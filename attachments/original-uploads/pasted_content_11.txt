PASS 2: VISUAL BRANDING & DESIGN POLISH
Now that the global consistency cleanup is complete, please make a careful visual branding pass across the Downsize Baltimore website.
IMPORTANT: Do not redesign the website. Do not rewrite the copy, change photographs, change page structure, or undo the consistency work completed in Pass 1. This pass is about refinement, not reinvention.
1. ANCHOR MOTIF
The anchor should be the primary subtle visual motif for Downsize Baltimore.
The anchor represents stability, guidance, home, Baltimore and Maryland's connection to the water, and Mary's philosophy of helping people feel grounded while deciding what comes next.
Review the decorative icons currently used throughout the website.
Where a compass is being used simply as decoration, consider replacing it with a tasteful, simple anchor.
Do NOT replace every icon with an anchor. Functional icons should still visually represent their subject.
The anchor should feel like a recurring brand detail, not a nautical theme park.
Use it sparingly in places such as:
section dividers
small decorative accents
occasional callout areas
footer details
Avoid oversized anchors, ropes, ship wheels, waves, or other literal nautical imagery.
2. COLOR BALANCE
Refine the existing color usage without changing the established palette.
The site should primarily feel:
warm + sophisticated + approachable + Baltimore
Keep the existing navy/blue family as an important grounding color.
Use warm cream, soft neutral and restrained warm accents to keep the website welcoming.
Please review areas where the tan/beige tones have become too dominant or muddy. Introduce more clean cream/white breathing room and navy where appropriate.
Do not make the site overly blue, overly beige, or overly feminine.
Avoid introducing additional colors that are not already part of the established visual direction.
3. VISUAL RHYTHM
Review the entire site for visual rhythm.
We want a thoughtful mix of:
photography
copy
white/cream space
occasional colored backgrounds
small branded details
Avoid having too many consecutive sections with the same background color or identical layout.
At the same time, do not create visual variety just for the sake of variety. The website should feel calm and intentional.
4. DECORATIVE ELEMENTS
Keep decorative graphics understated.
Downsize Baltimore should not feel like a generic senior-services website.
Avoid:
excessive stock-style illustrations
cliché aging imagery
excessive leaves/florals
random decorative icons
excessive nautical imagery
overly corporate graphic elements
Mary's photography and the stories should remain the personality of the website.
5. IMAGE PRESENTATION
Do not replace any existing photographs during this pass.
Review how existing photographs are displayed and make sure:
faces are not awkwardly cropped
images are not stretched or distorted
aspect ratios are preserved
mobile crops remain sensible
important portions of photographs remain visible
Some images may intentionally have different proportions. Do not force every photograph into the same shape.
6. KEEP THE BRAND HUMAN
The finished website should feel like someone is meeting Mary Lynch, not interacting with a large company.
Preserve the personal, editorial quality already established.
Professional does not need to mean corporate.
Warm does not need to mean sentimental.
Aging does not need to look old.
Downsizing does not need to look sad.
DO NOT CHANGE IN THIS PASS
Please do not:
rewrite website copy
add testimonials yet
build unfinished downloads
change the Meet Mary content
add missing Meet Mary photographs
redesign the Pop story
add the “3 A.M. and Unprepared” book content yet
alter the Downsizers Club content
create new pages
change navigation structure
replace existing photography
FINAL CHECK
When finished, review the website desktop and mobile.
Ask:
Does this feel like one distinctive Downsize Baltimore brand?
It should feel warm, grounded, knowledgeable, local and personal, with enough polish to reflect Mary's decades of real estate experience without losing the humanity that makes Downsize Baltimore different.
Preserve what is already working. Make restrained improvements only.
