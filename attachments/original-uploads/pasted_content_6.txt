# Buying & Selling Page Framework for Manus

## Overall feel

This page should feel polished, strategic, experienced, and easy to navigate.

It should show that once a client decides it is time to make a move, Mary can handle the actual real estate process at a very high level.

This page should feel different from Downsizing Services.

**Downsizing Services = planning, transition, reducing overwhelm.**

**Buying & Selling = execution, strategy, negotiation, timing, and getting the move done well.**

The page should not feel like a generic REALTOR page. It should feel specifically built for people making important housing decisions in the second half of life.

# SECTION 3: BUYING

## Headline

**Find the Home That Fits the Life You Want Now**

Buying is not simply about bedrooms, bathrooms, and square footage.

Mary helps clients think about both the real estate decision and the life surrounding it.

Questions may include:

* Do I want one-level living?
* How much maintenance do I want?
* Would I like to be closer to family?
* What happens if I drive less someday?
* Is healthcare nearby?
* Would I enjoy a more social community?
* Could this home adapt if my needs change?

## Buying options and specialties

### Rightsizing

Finding a smaller or easier home that better fits the way you want to live now.

### 55+ Communities and Condominiums

Helping clients compare community amenities, fees, housing styles, location, lifestyle, and long-term fit.

### One-Level and Accessible Living

Ranchers, villas, main-level primary suites, elevators, accessible homes, and properties with fewer physical barriers.

### Multigenerational Living

Homes that allow generations to live together while maintaining privacy, including separate entrances, living areas, kitchens, or bedroom suites.
