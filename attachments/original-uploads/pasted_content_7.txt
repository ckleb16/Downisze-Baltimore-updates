# CONTACT MARY

## Overall Direction

This page should feel **warm, calm, personal and easy**. It should not feel like a traditional real estate lead-generation page.

The primary goal is to encourage visitors to schedule a conversation with Mary through Calendly. The secondary option is a simple contact form or direct phone/email contact.

Keep the page uncluttered with plenty of breathing room.

---

## SECTION 1: HERO

### Headline

# Let's Talk About What Comes Next

### Copy

You don't need to have all the answers before you reach out.

Whether you're considering a move, helping someone you love, wondering if your current home still works for you, or simply trying to understand your options, let's start with a conversation.

### Primary CTA

**Schedule a 30-Minute Conversation With Mary**

Link this button directly to Mary's Calendly 30-minute appointment.

Under the button in smaller type:

**No pressure. No obligation. Just a place to start.**

### Hero Photo

Use the full-body photograph of Mary seated in the black cane chair, wearing the tan sweater and white pants.

Keep enough of the chair and residential setting visible to preserve the relaxed, conversational feeling of the photograph. Do not crop this into a traditional headshot.

The photo and copy can sit side by side on desktop and stack naturally on mobile.

## DESIGN NOTES

The page should feel like Mary is **inviting someone into a conversation**, not asking them to become a real estate lead.

Do not add testimonials, credentials, downloadable resources, property-search tools, lengthy service descriptions or additional calls to action.

Do not add multiple photographs of Mary. The seated tan-sweater photograph should be the primary and only significant image on this page.

The Calendly appointment is the primary conversion action and should appear before the contact form.

Keep the existing Downsize Baltimore visual language established on the homepage: warm, sophisticated, uncluttered, approachable and consistent with the "A Clear Plan for What Comes Next" brand.

The visitor should leave this page feeling:

**I don't have to know exactly what I need yet. I can just talk to Mary.**
