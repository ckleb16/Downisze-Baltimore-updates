# RESOURCE CENTER

## Mary Lynch | Downsize Baltimore | Cummings & Co. Realtors

## OVERALL PURPOSE

The Resource Center should feel like a **library of answers**, not a collection of downloads and not a directory of vendors.

Visitors often arrive here without knowing exactly what kind of professional or service they need. They may simply be wondering:

* Can I stay in my home?
* Should I think about downsizing?
* How do I help my parent?
* What do I do with a lifetime of belongings?
* What other housing options are available?
* I'm responsible for an estate. Where do I begin?

Organize the page around those real-life questions.

The overall feeling should be **helpful, calm, local, educational and easy to navigate**.

This page should be designed to grow over time as additional guides, FAQs, videos, articles and local resources are added.

# DESIGN DIRECTION

The Resource Center should feel like an **inviting library**, not a downloads warehouse.

Use the same warm, sophisticated Downsize Baltimore design language established throughout the website.

Prioritize:

* Questions over professional terminology
* Generous white space
* Easy scanning
* Simple icons or subtle visual cues
* Warm residential imagery where additional imagery is eventually needed
* Clear hierarchy
* Mobile usability
* Room for the Resource Center to expand substantially over time

Avoid:

* Stock photos of distressed older adults
* Fear-based imagery or copy
* Large vendor directories
* Walls of PDF links
* Too many competing CTAs
* Heavy email gating
* Overuse of Mary's credentials
* Making the page feel like a real estate lead funnel

The page should communicate:

**You don't need to know all the answers. You just need a good place to start.**
