# DOWNSIZE BALTIMORE

## FINAL HOME PAGE BUILD SHEET

### Overall Brand Direction

The Home page should feel:

* Sophisticated
* Warm
* Calm
* Local to Baltimore
* Approachable
* Experienced, but not corporate
* Senior-informed without looking like a “senior services” website

Primary brand colors are navy, cream/white, and gold.

Use nautical references **sparingly**. The anchor logo already establishes the theme. “Chart the Course” is an intentional subtle nod to the brand, not the beginning of a nautical theme throughout every section.

---

# DESIGN NOTES

* Avoid an overly “senior” visual identity.
* Avoid generic stock photographs of smiling older couples, moving boxes, caregivers, or people looking through photo albums.
* Keep generous white space.
* Use large, readable type.
* Buttons should be highly visible and consistent throughout.
* Navy should create depth and authority.
* Cream/white should keep the site warm and open.
* Gold should be used as an accent rather than dominating the page.
* Avoid clutter.
* Avoid excessive credentials on the Home page.
* Do not overuse nautical language or graphics.
* The website should feel polished but personal.
